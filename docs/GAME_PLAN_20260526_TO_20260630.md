## What to look for (the 6 hard signals)

If these are GREEN at 6/30, real-money go/no-go has the evidence
base it needs:

1. **Evidence count**: ≥30 closed trades since post_reset_20260522
   across the fleet
2. **Lifecycle hygiene**: zero ORPHAN_EXIT / DUPLICATE_ENTRY
3. **Health uptime**: daily_health_check worst_status ≤ YELLOW on
   ≥27 of 30 days
4. **Gate-band tracking**: each strategy's live_pf_30trades lands
   within its baseline CI band (or has a regime explanation)
5. **Restart-version cleanliness**: no runner spent >24h on an old
   git_sha during the window
6. **Auto_pause discipline**: every auto_pause recommendation
   applied within 24h

## What NOT to do

- **Don't extrapolate from a single week's PnL**. Per-strategy
  variance for n<10 trades is enormous; the CI bands are wide for
  a reason. Wait for ≥30 trades before claiming the strategy is
  "working" or "broken".
- **Don't raise allocation factors mid-month**. The 1× sizing was
  calibrated against the disciplined-gate slippage. Raising mid-
  month invalidates the live evidence under the new size.
- **Don't add new strategies**. The 5/31 freeze still holds — no
  new candidates until after 6/30. The hunt for new edges is
  resumed post-go-live.
- **Don't act on a single audit's findings without the agent
  cross-check**. Multi-agent design is intentional; one agent's
  blind spot is another's sweet spot.
