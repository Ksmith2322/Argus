# TRUTH-SURFACE DOCTRINE (architectural rule — never violate)

Argus has three truth classes:

  1. Canonical execution truth
     orders, fills, account, positions, decision events

  2. Deterministic lifecycle truth
     trade lifecycle records rebuilt from canonical truth (trade_journal)

  3. Analytical truth
     latency, slippage, attribution, equity curves, risk metrics, reports

Rules:
  - lower layers feed higher layers only
  - higher layers never mutate lower layers
  - analytics must never become hidden runtime control state
  - every derived metric must be traceable to its source artifact

# TRENDLINE ENGINE (Phase 18 feature, currently disabled)

  TrendlineEngine: detects pivot highs/lows on 1h candles, projects descending resistance
  and ascending support trendlines, computes proximity and breakout signals.
  Integrated into state.py, engine.py, confluence.py, decisions.py, config.py.

  Score effects (when USE_TRENDLINES=true):
    Near support trendline:      +5 to score (bonus)
    Near resistance trendline:   -8 to score (penalty)
    Broke above resistance:     +12 to score (bonus)
    Broke below support:        -15 to score + HARD BLOCK
    Resistance slope negative:   -5 to score (TL_PENALTY_RESIST_SLOPE_NEG)

  Status: USE_TRENDLINES=false. Bonuses were counterproductive in 2026-03-12 test.
  Next test: penalties only with TL_PENALTY_RESIST_SLOPE_NEG=5.
